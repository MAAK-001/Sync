# Northwind CRM Implementation Guide

## 1. Scope
Northwind CRM connects sales, service and billing data. Every implementation includes data migration of up to
two years of history, user training for up to 50 users, and a dedicated onboarding manager.

## 2. Timelines
Standard implementation takes 6-8 weeks from kickoff to go-live. Projects with more than two system integrations
take 10-12 weeks. A go-live date is only committed after the scoping workshop.

## 3. Integrations
Native connectors are available for SAP S/4HANA and SAP ECC (connector v4.2 and later), Salesforce and Microsoft
Dynamics. Legacy or custom billing systems are not supported out of the box and require a professional-services
engagement of 4-6 weeks. Oracle E-Business Suite is not supported.

## 4. Security and compliance
Northwind is ISO 27001 certified. SOC 2 Type II certification is in progress and expected next year.
Data can be hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) or India (Mumbai) regions. An annual third-party penetration test report is
available under NDA.

## 5. Support
Standard support covers weekdays 9am to 6pm IST. The response time for priority-one incidents is 8 hours on the
standard plan and 2 hours on the premium plan. Uptime commitment is 99.5% on standard and 99.9% on premium.
