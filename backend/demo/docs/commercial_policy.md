# Northwind Commercial Policy

## 1. Pricing
List price is 1,200 rupees per user per month, billed annually. The one-time implementation fee starts at 4 lakh.

## 2. Discounts
Account executives can offer a discount of up to 10% for a two-year contract. Any discount above 10% needs approval
from the regional sales director.

## 3. Payment terms
Standard payment terms are net 30 days from invoice. Annual billing is paid in advance.

## 4. Refunds and exit
Refunds are limited to 50% of the unused annual fee when cancelled within the first 90 days. Contracts can be ended
with 90 days' notice; customer data export is included free of charge.
